<Hook>

Two model ids are in the notes, the gate is red, and the chat says the feature is ready. The notes are the assignment. The gate is the result. The chat is neither.

</Hook>

## The idea

Strong and fast are roles. The strong role edits the spec and reviews. The fast role takes one card. Claude and Grok are family names. The billable choice is an id such as `claude-opus-5-5`, `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`, or `grok-4.7`, with the prices and context windows Part 1 recorded for September 2026. A live vendor page that disagrees wins.

Price, the clock you measure, and the exit code are the triangle. A public benchmark is a hypothesis. Your three cards are the test. Plan, build, and review are separate sessions. The reviewer from the other family sees the diff and the spec, not the builder's summary.

A blocked card escalates with that packet. It does not escalate as "fix the app" on a more expensive <Term id="llm">LLM</Term>. A day ends with commits that passed the gate, or with an honest blocked note. Mixing fails when the spec is wrong, the window is too small, or two diffs are mashed together.

An <Term id="agent">agent</Term> does not outrank the spec because you paid more for it.
